# Privacy policy

**Cart Price Changes for Amazon** runs entirely inside your browser.

- It collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere. There is no server,
  no analytics, no account and no network request of its own. The only images
  it loads are product pictures from Amazon's own image service, the same way
  the Amazon page loads them.
- It reads the Amazon cart page you are looking at: the price-change notices
  Amazon shows, and the name, price and picture of the items in your cart and
  saved-for-later list.
- It stores what it reads in your browser's local storage for the Amazon site
  you use (for example `www.amazon.com.tr`), together with your view, sort and
  period preferences. Nothing is synced. Entries older than a year are dropped.
  **Clear history** in the panel's footer deletes everything it stored, and
  removing the extension does the same.
- It requests no browser permissions. It runs only on Amazon storefront pages.

Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/Coursion-Studio/amazon-cart-price-changes/issues.
